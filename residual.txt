D-1  other_players == attackers - 1 in 213 of 213 records, without exception.
     97 of those fights had more than one of OUR characters present, so the
     field counts one of ours as an outsider. Either `attackers` undercounts by one
     when two of our logs merge, or `other_players` overcounts by one. Both cannot hold.
     shown in the worked example itself: Bazzt Zzzt D0 16 Aug 2026, observers ['Avenrae', 'Shara'], attackers 2, other_players 1
D-2  10 records list the same observer name more than once (('Avenrae', 'Shara', 'Shara') and similar).
     If `observers` is a character list this is a duplicate; if it is a log-file list
     the name is not unique enough to divide by. Either way a per-character figure
     cannot be derived from it without knowing which. Not a number I will guess.

     error: it is consistent with `seconds` spanning OUR witnessed lines while
     `joined_late_seconds` is measured from the boss's first engagement by anyone.

     the two cannot be added. Which they are decides whether a DPS denominator
     built from `seconds` is our engaged window or the raid's. A question, not a claim.
